My home page is a white page with a pixel garden. Tap anywhere and a flower stitches itself in, pixel by pixel. Here's how a few of the small parts work.

## It only draws when something moves

The garden is a single `<canvas>`, drawn in stepped frames like real pixel art. When nothing is growing, flying or unpicking, it stops drawing completely. An idle garden costs nothing, which matters on a phone that's been sitting open in a tab.

## Every tap is in tune

All the sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files to download. Everything plays in one key, D major pentatonic. That scale has no notes that clash, so any mix of taps, in any order, comes out sounding like music.

```js
// D major pentatonic. degree 0 is D5; negative degrees go down, 5 is D6
const PENTA = [0, 2, 4, 7, 9];
const ROOT = 74;
const hz = m => 440 * 2 ** ((m - 69) / 12);
const deg = i => hz(ROOT + PENTA[((i % 5) + 5) % 5] + 12 * Math.floor(i / 5));
```

Planting walks a small melody on a kalimba-like tone. A growing flower plays a music-box run, and its sparkles chime an octave up.

## It makes room on its own

A garden that only grows ends up as a wall of flowers. So once it passes 40 flowers (24 on a phone), the oldest one unpicks itself over about three seconds, and another goes a few seconds later. It never clears the moment a new flower lands, so you always see what you just planted.

## Calm when you ask for calm

Every animation has a reduced-motion version. With reduced motion on, flowers appear without stitching, butterflies sit still and the scroll effects are off. The garden still works. It just stops moving.
